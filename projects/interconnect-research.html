01 / RESEARCH QUESTION

Interconnects beyond copper

This research compares multi-layer graphene nanoribbon (MLGNR) and multi-walled carbon nanotube (MWCNT) interconnects against conventional copper, focusing on electrical performance at scaled technology nodes.

The research material describes analytical RLC/ESC modeling and HSPICE-based simulation, with Cosmoscope used to inspect simulation behavior.

04 / REPORTED FINDINGS

Selected comparison results

The paper abstract reports that MLGNR has lower delay than MWCNT at a 10 μm interconnect length, with reductions of 13.38% at 7 nm and 14.34% at 5 nm. The manuscript also reports up to 11.25% lower PDP at 10 μm. In the detailed discussion, another delay figure of 10.94% appears; this differs from the abstract's 5 nm figure and should be reconciled against the source plots before using that particular value.

05 / RESEARCH VISUALS

Figures and supporting files

The source archive includes the thesis, the paper draft, calculation workbooks (`mcp_calculation.xlsx`, `Vlsi cnt cal.xlsx`, and `Vlsi.xlsx`) and presentation diagrams. The study evaluates interconnect lengths from 1 μm to 10 μm and uses HSPICE to compare delay, power dissipation and PDP. The calculation workbook also records interconnect parasitics such as resistance, kinetic and magnetic inductance, and capacitance.
